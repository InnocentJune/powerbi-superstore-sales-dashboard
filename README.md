🛒 Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)
📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates my ability to design and develop interactive business intelligence dashboards using Power BI. The dashboard analyzes sales performance from the Sample Superstore dataset, providing insights into revenue generation, profitability, customer segments, product categories, shipping modes, and regional trends.

The goal of the dashboard is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that support strategic decision-making.

🔑 Key Features
📊 Executive KPIs
Total Sales: $2.30M
Total Profit: $286.40K
Total Orders: 9.99K
Average Sales: $229.86
Profit Margin: 12.47%
🌍 Regional Analysis
Compare sales performance across Central, East, South, and West regions.
Identify the most profitable regions.
👥 Customer Segment Analysis
Analyze profitability across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
Understand customer contribution to overall profit.
📦 Product Performance
Detailed sub-category analysis showing both sales and profit.
Identify top-performing and underperforming products.
🚚 Shipping Mode Analysis
Evaluate profitability across shipping methods.
Compare First Class, Second Class, Standard Class, and Same Day shipping performance.
🗺️ State-Level Sales Analysis
Visualize sales distribution by state.
Identify high-performing geographic markets.
🎛️ Interactive Filtering

Users can explore the dashboard through slicers for:

Region
Segment
Category
Ship Mode
📈 Key Insights
The West Region generated the highest sales revenue.
The Consumer Segment contributed the highest profit.
Standard Class Shipping produced the strongest profit performance.
Technology products emerged as one of the leading sales categories.
California recorded the highest sales among states.
